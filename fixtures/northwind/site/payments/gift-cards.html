Gift cards

Northwind gift cards are available in amounts from $25 to $500, as a printed card or as an email gift card delivered on the date you choose.

Using a gift card

Enter the 16-digit card number and 4-digit PIN at checkout. You can combine up to three gift cards on one order and pay any remaining balance with another payment method.

Checking your balance

Your remaining balance is shown at checkout after you enter the card. Northwind gift cards never expire and have no fees.

Lost gift cards

We can replace a lost gift card only if you have the original order number used to buy it. Gift cards cannot be returned or exchanged for cash, except where required by law.
