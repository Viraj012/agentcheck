Payment methods and billing

Accepted payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, Apple Pay and Northwind gift cards. We do not accept checks, cash on delivery or cryptocurrency.

When you are charged

We authorize your card when you place the order and capture the payment when the order ships. If an order ships in more than one package, you may see a separate charge for each package.

Sales tax

Sales tax is calculated at checkout based on your shipping address and the tax rules of that state.

Price adjustments

If an item you bought drops in price within 14 days of your order, contact us and we will refund the difference once per item.
