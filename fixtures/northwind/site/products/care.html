Caring for your Northwind products

Cast iron

Rinse cast iron with hot water while it is still warm and scrub with a stiff brush. Avoid soaking it or putting it in the dishwasher. Dry it on the stove over low heat, then rub in a thin layer of vegetable oil to keep the seasoning strong.

Wooden cutting boards

Hand wash wooden boards with mild soap and dry them standing on edge. Once a month, rub in food-safe mineral oil. Never soak a wooden board or put it in the dishwasher, because it will warp and crack.

Enameled cookware

Use wooden or silicone utensils on enameled pots, and let them cool before washing to avoid cracking the enamel.
