Creating and managing your account

A Northwind account lets you track orders, start returns, save addresses and reorder favorites. You can also check out as a guest without one.

Creating an account

Choose Sign in at the top of any page, then Create account. Enter your name, email address and a password of at least 10 characters. We send a confirmation link to verify your email.

Resetting your password

On the sign-in page choose Forgot password and enter your email address. The reset link we email you expires after 60 minutes. If it has expired, request a new one.

Saved addresses

Open Account settings and choose Addresses to add, edit or remove shipping addresses. You can mark one address as your default.

Closing your account

To close your account, contact support from the email address on the account. Closing an account does not cancel orders that have already shipped.
