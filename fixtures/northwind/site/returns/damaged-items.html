Damaged, defective or wrong items

We are sorry if something arrived broken or isn't what you ordered. Please report the problem within 7 days of delivery so we can make it right.

What to send us

Email support with your order number, a short description, and photos of the item and the shipping box. Keep the packaging until your claim is resolved, because the carrier may ask to inspect it.

What happens next

For damaged or defective items we send a free replacement or, if it is out of stock, a full refund including the original shipping cost. You do not need to return broken glass or ceramics; we will tell you how to dispose of them safely.

If you received the wrong item, we email you a prepaid label for the wrong item and ship the correct one right away.
