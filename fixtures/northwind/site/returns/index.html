Our return policy

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a refund to your original payment method. Items must be unused and in their original packaging.

How to start a return

  1. Sign in to your account, open Orders and choose Return items next to the order.
  2. Select the items and a reason. We email you a prepaid return label.
  3. Pack the items securely, attach the label and drop the parcel at any UPS location.

Return shipping is free for defective or incorrect items. For all other returns, a $6.95 return shipping fee is deducted from your refund.

Items that cannot be returned

Opened pantry goods, gift cards, and items marked Final Sale cannot be returned.

After we receive your return

Our warehouse team inspects every returned item. Once your return is approved, we issue your refund to the original payment method and send you an email confirmation.
