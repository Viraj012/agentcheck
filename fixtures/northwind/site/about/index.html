About Northwind Supply Co.

Our story

Northwind started in 2011 as a single stall at the Portland farmers market selling hand-seasoned cast iron. Today we ship cookware, tools and pantry goods across the United States from our Portland warehouse.

Our sustainability commitment

We ship in recycled cardboard with paper padding and no plastic air pillows. Since 2022 our warehouse has run on renewable electricity.

Because we keep as little customer data as possible, account changes go through people rather than a settings page. To change the email address on your account, email support from your current address with the new address you want to use; the change takes effect within one business day and we send a confirmation to both addresses.

Our team

Forty-two people work at Northwind, from warehouse packers to the support team who answer your emails. Many of us are home cooks, and we test every product in our own kitchens before we sell it.
