Shipping options and delivery times

Processing time

Orders placed before 1 p.m. Pacific Time on a business day are packed and handed to the carrier the same day. Orders placed after 1 p.m., on weekends or on public holidays ship the next business day.

Shipping methods and costs

We offer three shipping methods within the contiguous United States:

  • Standard Ground: $5.99, delivered in 3 to 5 business days. Free on orders of $50 or more.
  • Expedited: $12.99, delivered in 2 business days.
  • Overnight: $24.99, delivered the next business day when ordered before 1 p.m. Pacific Time.

Orders to Alaska and Hawaii ship by Standard Ground only and take 5 to 9 business days. A $10 surcharge applies.

Heavy and oversized items

Cast iron sets and items over 30 pounds always ship by Standard Ground, even if you choose a faster method, and do not qualify for free shipping.

PO boxes

We can ship to PO boxes by Standard Ground only. Oversized items cannot be delivered to a PO box.
