Tracking your order

As soon as your order leaves our warehouse we email you a shipping confirmation with a tracking number and a link to the carrier's tracking page. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to appear after you receive the email.

Finding your tracking number later

Sign in to your Northwind account and open Orders. Each shipped order shows its carrier and tracking number. If you checked out as a guest, use the link in your confirmation email instead.

My package shows delivered but I can't find it

Check around your property and with neighbors, and wait one more business day because carriers sometimes mark packages delivered early. If it still has not arrived, contact us within 14 days of the delivery date and we will open a claim with the carrier.
